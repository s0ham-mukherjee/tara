Technical depth matters. So does the ability to work across the vehicle. Explore the six departments that make the mission possible.

DIV / 01

Propulsion

Learn the fundamentals of thrust, motor systems, grain geometry, performance modelling and responsible handling.

DIV / 02

Aerodynamics

Shape the flight through stability analysis, drag estimation, simulation and aerodynamic testing.

DIV / 03

Structures

Translate loads into a manufacturable airframe using materials, joints, interfaces and verification.

DIV / 04

Avionics

Build the rocket’s nervous system: sensing, telemetry, power, data capture and flight computers.

DIV / 05

Recovery & Launch

Own deployment architecture, recovery systems, range operations and a safe, repeatable launch day.

DIV / 06

Systems & Safety

Integrate the vehicle, manage interfaces, run reviews and make safety the team’s shared language.

Tell us what you want to learn, what you can already do and where you think you could add value. We will route your interest to the right team.

Who can apply?

Students at TIET who are ready to contribute consistently. Different departments and years bring different strengths to the programme.

Do I need prior rocket experience?

No. A strong learning mindset, attention to detail and respect for process matter more than a perfect starting point.

What does commitment look like?

TARA is built around dependable ownership. Expect regular team work, documentation and review cycles alongside your academics.
