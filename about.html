TIET / PATIALA FORMING 2026 — MISSION 001 FIRST VEHICLE PRINTED · NEARING LAUNCH
TARA turns fragmented classroom learning into one visible, outcome-driven student project.

To establish a premier, student-led aerospace ecosystem at TIET that bridges academic engineering and real-world aerospace applications.

The initiative fosters interdisciplinary collaboration, design and build capability, leadership pipelines and the confidence to operate with discipline — with the long-term goal of positioning TIET as a competitive hub for national and international rocketry.

A culture with
altitude.

The engineering is demanding. The culture makes it possible.

Principle 01

Ownership

Students own meaningful work, make decisions with context and leave behind a stronger system for the next cohort.

Principle 02

Rigor

Every claim earns evidence. Reviews, records and test data turn good intent into dependable engineering.

Principle 03

Care

Safety, respect and responsible operations are not constraints on ambition. They are how ambition lasts.

One programme.
Many trajectories.
