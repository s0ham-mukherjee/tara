The mission architecture

Earn the
next milestone.

The TARA programme makes progress visible. Each stage creates artefacts that support the next stage of engineering and leadership.

PHASE 01 · NOW

Stand up the society

Charter, leadership pipeline, department mandates, safety framework and institutional alignment.

PHASE 02

Build the capability

Onboarding, fundamentals, toolchains, design standards and subsystem requirements.

PHASE 03

Design & verify

Preliminary and critical design reviews, manufacturing, ground testing and integrated test readiness.

PHASE 04

Launch & learn

Range planning, safe operations, flight data and a documented path to the next vehicle.

Operating principle

Make it legible.
