The case for TARA
One visible, outcome-driven project can turn fragmented learning into applied excellence.

TARA is being established as a formally recognized technical society at TIET. It is designed to bridge academic engineering and real-world aerospace applications through disciplined, interdisciplinary work.

Systems thinking

See the full vehicle, not just the component. Integrate propulsion, avionics, structures and recovery as one accountable system.

02 /

Documented processes

Design reviews, test evidence and clear ownership make every iteration safer, faster and easier to learn from.

Responsible operations
